Posted · demo

Two days.

One was public. One wasn’t. Both counted.

Demo build · decisions taken as defaults

Spec §6 answers baked into this build

  • Q1 Movement: click-to-interact, plus a cosmetic walk (HTML can; Figma can’t).
  • Q2 Retry: free, restores day-start state. Flagged: free retry weakens consequences — decide before Figma.
  • Q3 Payout: F pays 25, C pays 40, C+ pays 50, A pays 75 (kindness pays more — the conventional choice). Day 2 pays on the same scale.
  • Q4 Grade: from the dialogue outcome only. “Fk Drake” = F, a simple sorry = C+, apologizing to Drake in person = A — unless the apology comes with an “if”, which lands a C (a bad apology in person can sting more than none).
  • Q5 Hearts: John starts at 3/5 (neutral).
  • Q6 Drake: now on screen — he sits in the front row. He still never confronts you; the A choice walks over to him.
  • Classroom arrival (new): ~12s free-roam window before John approaches — tap anywhere on the classroom floor to walk there (paths route around the desks); two students gossip about Drake in the meantime.
  • Q7 Shop items: cosmetic decor only. The room also has fixed decor now: rug, runner, posters, and a bulletin board.
  • Bulletin board (new): click the corkboard on the bedroom wall to see friendship hearts with John, Drake, and Alex, plus an overall kindness score.
  • Q8 Shop: skippable — you can go straight to bed.
  • Q9: exactly one confrontation per day.
  • Q10: Day 2 is a stub proving the loop closes.
  • §7: post option 3 replaced with a visible “cut for demo” slot.
  • Day 2 (v0.2 addendum): typed replies, now a two-stage conversation — stage 1 matches “same lol” / “are you okay”; stage 2 matches supportive lines (“that sucks”, “wanna talk about it?”) or brush-offs (“lol”). Deflecting first gets one chance to circle back; asking then brushing off drops to a B. Real AI interpretation is the next build. Now graded — asked = A (+75), joked along = C (+40), left on read = F (+25). The DM replaces the public post. Alex is one-and-done, but now gets a reaction cutscene at his screen.
  • Day 2 timing note: leave-on-read fires after 10s of no typing; starting to type cancels it.
  • Day 2 retry: the reflection card now has Retry — an override of the addendum’s “you can’t retry noticing” stance, by request. Rewinds to the morning DM.
  • Cutscene: Alex’s inner thought appears as a bubble — crying if brushed off, beaming if asked. Ignoring leaves his silence unnarrated on purpose.
  • Movement: click anywhere on the bedroom floor to walk there; the PC still opens Chirper. At night, going to bed walks over, climbs in, and falls asleep (💤) before the day turns.
